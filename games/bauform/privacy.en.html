This policy covers Bauform for Android, version 1.0: free, with no ads, in-app purchases or user accounts.

Data on your device

Bauform stores your best score, sound and vibration preferences, tutorial status and current game in its private WebView storage. A saved game contains a local game ID, random seed, moves, board proportions, start time, play duration and initial best score. Results of the last twenty games are stored locally for diagnostics: date, duration, score and shape and piece counts.

These values support game resumption, preferences and results. They are not sent to the publisher, sold or used for advertising. The app contains no remote analytics, advertising or crash-reporting service.

Sharing by choice

When you select Share, the app creates a PNG containing your composition, composition number and score in its private cache, then opens Android's share sheet. You choose the receiving app and recipient. Bauform does not select a recipient or upload the image to a publisher server. The service you choose follows its own policy. The temporary image may remain cached until Android or you clear it.

Permissions and offline use

VIBRATE enables optional vibration feedback. INTERNET is declared for the Capacitor environment; the bundled game, fonts and sounds work without requests to a server. Bauform does not request access to contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos or personal files. Sharing grants temporary access only to the poster you choose to share.

Retention and deletion

The current save is updated after moves and removed when a game ends or restarts. Only the last twenty results are retained. Best score and preferences remain until you clear the app's data or uninstall it. Android automatic backup is disabled in the app configuration; manufacturer-managed device transfer may vary. Use Android Settings → Apps → Bauform → Storage → Clear data to erase local data. Clearing the cache alone removes temporary images, not progress.

Contact and rights

Privacy enquiries: contact@gardenofearth.games. If you contact support, the information you choose to send, such as your email address and message, is used to answer your request. Do not include sensitive information. You may request access, correction or deletion of correspondence under applicable law. Correspondence is processed on the basis of the legitimate interest in answering requests, by the publisher and its email service provider. Planned retention is the time needed to handle the request and no more than twelve months after closure, unless a legal obligation requires otherwise. You may complain to your local data protection authority, including the CNIL in France.

The game does not collect age or include personalised advertising. This policy will be updated if the app's practices change.
